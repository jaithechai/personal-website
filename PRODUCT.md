# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
- Hiring engineers and recruiters reviewing Summer 2027 internship candidates in electrical, computer, embedded, hardware, controls, firmware and software roles. They arrive from a resume link and give it under a minute.
- Professors running NSF REU Sites, checking whether the applicant has done real technical work.

## Purpose
Prove in seconds that Jai Dilbaghi designs real circuits, builds them and debugs them, then make the proof easy to dig into. Success is a reader who opens a project and then gets in touch.

## Brand personality
Soldered, load-tested, plainspoken. Confident because the evidence is specific, not because the adjectives are big.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI portfolios: cream background, display serif, small uppercase mono eyebrows, soft fade-ins, centered hero.
- Dark glowing gradient templates and glass cards.
- Empty slogans such as "Builder. Researcher." or "passionate about technology". Every line must carry a fact.
- A costume. The schematic concept must never make facts harder to find. Plain headings stay plain.

## Design principles
1. Evidence first: real numbers, real parts, real photos, real links.
2. The site is one schematic sheet: wires, symbols and a title block carry meaning and are not decoration.
3. Scannable in 30 seconds, rewarding in 3 minutes.
4. Works without JavaScript and without motion. Motion only adds to content that is already visible.

## Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard access, visible focus, prefers-reduced-motion honored, usable at 375px wide.

## Standing content rules
- No class year, graduation date, or "freshman" or "sophomore" anywhere, by Jai's decision.
- No resume link or button, by Jai's decision.
- Never claim the telemetry dashboard software as Jai's work. Jai built the telemetry hardware.
- The Agentic Personal Operations System copy carries no metrics and never says it applies or submits.
- Current facts come from Jai's resume. When the site and the resume disagree, the resume wins.
